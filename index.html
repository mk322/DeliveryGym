Deliver food under real-world constraints

Choose among orders A–D, collect food, plan a route, and hand it to the customer. Every decision must account for six coupled constraints: time, location, energy, money, food condition, and safety.

RL training loop · Figure 2

Train on actions and outcomes in UE5

Observe the world → choose a tool action → execute it in UE5 → verify the outcome. Payments, costs, food condition, and safety events ground the reward used to update the policy with online RL.

Adaptive curriculum

Adaptive environment for embodied RSI

Can the environment make RL more effective? DeliveryGym evaluates the current policy, identifies weak capabilities, and generates feasible tasks that target them. Policy improvement changes the next round of training—an environment-driven loop towards embodied recursive self-improvement (RSI).

Task and map scalability

A scalable environment for embodied RL

DeliveryGym scales RL training across diverse task configurations and city maps. Expanding both dimensions produces stronger delivery policies, with gains demonstrated under a fixed budget of 2,400 training trajectories in each study.
