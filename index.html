Why an RL environment?

A benchmark measures behavior. An RL environment improves it.

Learning requires more than a task and a score. The environment must define observations, executable actions, state transitions, and grounded feedback that can support repeated policy updates.

▶

Executable dynamics

UE5 determines how each tool action changes location, time, resources, traffic, orders, and the next observation.

✓

Native verification

Simulator events verify outcomes, costs, and safety without action labels or an LLM judge.

↗

Online RL system

Resettable parallel workers, batched inference, and complete trajectories support repeated policy improvement.

↻

Adaptive tasks

Training configurations shift toward measured weaknesses while evaluation remains fixed.

Embodied task

A courier task designed for learning

The agent receives multimodal context, chooses a structured tool action, observes the executed consequence, and continues from the resulting state.

01

Observe

Egocentric RGB, route map, orders, resources, and prior action result.

02

Act

Select orders, navigate, collect, deliver, wait, rest, or recharge.

03

Transition

UE5 executes the action and updates physical and operational state.

04

Verify

Event traces produce grounded rewards and the next training transition.

Adaptive curriculum

Training tasks adapt as the policy improves
