Task

A complete courier shift, one turn at a time

DeliveryGym turns delivery work into an executable embodied task. The agent observes the world, chooses a tool action, receives the UE5 transition and verifier feedback, then continues from the resulting state.

01

Observe

Egocentric RGB, route map, orders, time, resources, and prior action result.

02

Choose work

Inspect, accept, or reject available orders and decide what matters next.

03

Act in UE5

Navigate, collect, hand over, wait, rest, or recharge through structured tools.

04

Verify & continue

Executed events update the ledger, constraints, reward, and the next observation.

Overview

Why environment-side self-improvement?

Agent learning is limited by the experience an environment provides. DeliveryGym closes that loop: agent behavior reveals a weakness, and the environment changes future practice to target it.

∞

Persistent shifts

Orders share one clock, location, resource budget, and earnings ledger. Earlier decisions change later opportunities.

✓

Verifiable RL feedback

Simulator events independently verify delivery outcomes, economic returns, resource costs, and safety failures.

↻

Adaptive curriculum

Fixed probes identify changing weaknesses; the environment targets future training toward the agent’s capability frontier.

Architecture

A closed loop between experience and learning

Online rollouts produce simulator-verified returns. Policy updates change behavior; training probes measure what remains hard; adaptive sampling changes what the agent experiences next.

Read street views, a route map, and structured state—including the resources and commitments left by earlier decisions.
