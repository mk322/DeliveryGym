DeliveryGym: An RL Environment for Long-Horizon Embodied Agent Planning with Adaptive Curriculum

DeliveryGym is a UE5 training environment for delivery agents: actions execute in the world, simulator events ground RL feedback, and adaptive tasks change what the policy practices as it improves.

Adaptive environment for embodied RSI

The environment changes what the policy practices next

Training-only probes evaluate traffic compliance, obstacle recovery, and multi-order planning. DeliveryGym identifies the weakest capability, increases targeted practice, verifies that each generated shift remains feasible, and continues online RL.
