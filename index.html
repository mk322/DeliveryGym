The idea

Persistent consequences.
Verifiable feedback.

DeliveryGym turns continuous courier shifts into an executable learning environment for embodied agents. Each decision changes the time, energy, money, and order commitments available for later work. Agents observe street views, maps, and structured state, then use tools to choose orders, navigate, deliver, and recover resources. Simulator events verify what happened and provide trajectory rewards for reinforcement learning. Across 13 city maps, evaluation reveals that reliable single-order execution does not guarantee profitable shift-level planning. Training Qwen3-VL-4B with safety-aware rewards improves test income by 54.3% over initialization. In a separate comparison with the same rollout budget, an adaptive curriculum improves test income by 16.5% over uniform sampling. The curriculum changes future training tasks to target measured weaknesses; evaluation remains fixed. Together, these results connect persistent consequences, verifiable feedback, and targeted practice in one environment.
